# 推销话术模板（英文原文 + 中文说明）

> 使用前我会根据具体行业和客户替换 `{花括号}` 里的内容。你只需复制发送。

## 1. 第一封邮件（附样品链接）

**中文说明**：简短、只讲对方的问题、直接给样品，不要求对方先付出时间。

```
Subject: made a new site for {Business Name}

Hi {First Name},

I was looking for a {service} company in {City} and found {Business Name} on Google.
Your reviews are great ({rating} stars), but your website {specific problem: is hard to use on a phone / has no way to book online / doesn't load}.

So I built you a new version, free to look at:
{demo link}

It includes online booking and a chat assistant that answers customers 24/7.

If you like it, I can make it live this week. If not, no problem at all.

{Your Name}
{Company Name} · {Address}
Reply "no" and I won't email again.
```

## 2. 跟进邮件（3 天后没回复）

```
Subject: re: made a new site for {Business Name}

Hi {First Name}, just checking you saw the demo I made for you:
{demo link}

Most {service} owners I talk to say they miss calls while on a job.
This version texts the customer back automatically when you can't pick up.

Worth a quick 10-minute call?

{Your Name}
```

## 3. 最后一封（再过 4 天）

```
Subject: should I take it down?

Hi {First Name}, I'll keep the demo for {Business Name} online until Friday, then remove it.
{demo link}

If the timing is wrong, just reply "later" and I'll check back next month.

{Your Name}
```

## 4. 短信 / 社交私信版（更短）

```
Hi {First Name}, I made a free new website mockup for {Business Name}: {demo link}
Want me to make it live? No worries if not. – {Your Name}
```

## 5. 通话话术（15 分钟）

| 步骤 | 说什么（英文） | 中文目的 |
|---|---|---|
| 开场 | "Thanks for taking the call. Did you get a chance to look at the demo?" | 确认对方看过样品 |
| 了解问题 | "How do most of your customers find you right now? Do you ever miss calls while you're on a job?" | 让对方自己说出痛点 |
| 展示 | 共享屏幕，打开样品，演示手机版、预约、AI 客服 | 让对方看到具体效果 |
| 报价 | "To make it live it's $997 one-time. Most clients pick the $1,997 package with booking and missed-call text-back, plus $197 a month for hosting and updates." | 先给低价锚点，再推主套餐 |
| 处理顾虑 | 见下表 | |
| 成交 | "If you're happy, I'll send the payment link now and it'll be live within 3 business days." | 当场发付款链接 |

**常见顾虑回答：**

| 客户说 | 回答 |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive." | "Understood. One extra job a month pays for it. We can start with the $997 version and add the rest later." |
| "I already have a guy." | "No problem. Feel free to keep the demo and show it to them." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What would you need to see to feel sure? I can hold this price until Friday." |
| "Is this a scam?" | "Fair question. You only pay after you approve the final site, and here's our company page: {link}." |

## 6. 收款建议

- 小客户：发 PayPal / Stripe 付款链接，**先收 50% 定金**，上线前收尾款。
- $1,000 以下：可以一次付清，上线前允许无限次小修改，降低对方顾虑。
